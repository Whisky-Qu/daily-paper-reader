## 中文摘要

近年来，具有灵活的运动功能的人形机器人引起了很大的关注.人类运动的模仿已被证明是他们学习的有效方法.这篇文章介绍了一个新的培训框架，叫做从人类分解模仿到人形机器人 (DIH2H).我们定义了一个"关键技能集"来代表人类的基本运动.通过引入行动重定向机制， 代理学习关节运动模式， 而不是直接复制人类运动.这种专注的方法使人形机器人能够有效地处理它自己的运动和人类步态之间的差异，从而防止不自然的运动.对机器人的上部和下部进行了单独训练.下部使用模仿学习 (IL)，而上部利用IL和课程学习的结合.通过逐步引入越来越具挑战性的任务和多样化的环境，上部可以强大地适应各种地形和运动的动态变化.模拟结果表明，DIH2H在各种任务和环境中提高了政策学习效率和通用化，实现了10%的模仿精度和25%的更高的数据效率。

## Abstract

In recent years, humanoid robots with flexible locomotion capabilities have attracted significant attention. Imitation from human motion has proven to be an effective approach for them to learn. This article presents a novel training framework called Decomposed Imitation from Human to Humanoid (DIH2H). We define a “key skill set” to represent essential human motions. By introducing an action redirection mechanism, an agent learns joint motion patterns rather than directly copying human motions. This focused approach allows a humanoid robot to effectively handle the differences between its own locomotion and human gaits, thereby preventing unnatural motion. The control policy is trained separately for the robot’s upper and lower body. The lower body employs imitation learning (IL), while the upper one leverages a combination of IL and curriculum learning. By gradually introducing increasingly challenging tasks and diverse environments, the upper body can robustly adapt to dynamic changes across various terrains and motions. Simulation results demonstrate that DIH2H improves policy learning efficiency and generalization across diverse tasks and environments, achieving a 10% increase in imitation accuracy and 25% higher data efficiency over its existing peers.
