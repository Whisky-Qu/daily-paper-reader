## 中文摘要

摘要 通过下肢 外骨骼为人体提供顺利和自然的辅助至关重要.然而，一个重大挑战是确定各种运动模式，使外骨骼能够提供无套的支持.在本研究中，我们提出了一种为运动模式识别的方法，叫做Conv-ViT (Conv-Enhanced Vision Transformer).这种方法最大限度地利用卷积对特征提取和融合的好处，以及变压器的自我注意机制，以有效地捕捉和处理输入序列内的不同位置之间的长期依赖性.通过配备外骨骼的惯性测量单元，我们收集了27个健康实体的运动数据，为了确保外骨骼在各种运动模式之间的过渡期间的稳定性和安全性，我们不仅检查了典型的五种稳定模式 (涉及平地步行 [WL]，楼梯登山 [SA]，楼梯登山 [SD]，坡梯登山 [RA]和坡梯登山 [RD]) ，还广泛探索了八个运动过渡 (包括WL-SA，WL-SD，WL-RA，WL-RD，SA-WL，SD-WL，RA-WL，RD-WL).在5个稳定的运动和8个过渡的任务中，识别精度分别达到98.87%和96.74%.与三个受欢迎的算法 ViT，卷积神经网络和支持向量机相比，结果表明，拟议的方法具有最好的识别性能，与其他方法相比，在准确性和F1分数方面存在非常显著的差异.最后，我们还证明了Conv-ViT在通用性方面表现出色。

## Abstract

Abstract Providing the human body with smooth and natural assistance through lower limb exoskeletons is crucial. However, a significant challenge is identifying various locomotion modes to enable the exoskeleton to offer seamless support. In this study, we propose a method for locomotion mode recognition named Convolution‐enhanced Vision Transformer (Conv‐ViT). This method maximizes the benefits of convolution for feature extraction and fusion, as well as the self‐attention mechanism of the Transformer, to efficiently capture and handle long‐term dependencies among different positions within the input sequence. By equipping the exoskeleton with inertial measurement units, we collected motion data from 27 healthy subjects, using it as input to train the Conv‐ViT model. To ensure the exoskeleton&#x27;s stability and safety during transitions between various locomotion modes, we not only examined the typical five steady modes (involving walking on level ground [WL], stair ascent [SA], stair descent [SD], ramp ascent [RA], and ramp descent [RD]) but also extensively explored eight locomotion transitions (including WL‐SA, WL‐SD, WL‐RA, WL‐RD, SA‐WL, SD‐WL, RA‐WL, RD‐WL). In tasks involving the recognition of five steady locomotions and eight transitions, the recognition accuracy reached 98.87% and 96.74%, respectively. Compared with three popular algorithms, ViT, convolutional neural networks, and support vector machine, the results show that the proposed method has the best recognition performance, and there are highly significant differences in accuracy and F1 score compared to other methods. Finally, we also demonstrated the excellent performance of Conv‐ViT in terms of generalization performance.
