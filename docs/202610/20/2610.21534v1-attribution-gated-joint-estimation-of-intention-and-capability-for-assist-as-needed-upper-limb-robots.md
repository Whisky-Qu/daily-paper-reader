## 摘要
按需辅助（AAN）控制必须区分预期运动的变化与有效自主力矩能力的变化，尽管二者可能产生相似的运动学和交互响应。提出一种双测量量的多模态 MHE-MPC 架构作为基础框架，其中预期轨迹和任务相关的有效自主力矩能力作为不同量进行在线估计，并利用这两个估计自适应调整机器人辅助。在该架构基础上，将两个更新通道之间的歧义建模为连续信用分配问题，并通过归因门控加以解决。轨迹变化指标由持续纠正性交互获得，而能力变化指标则由归一化肌肉激活与动力学重构自主力矩之间的残差导出。九名健康参与者完成了目标高度-负载析因试验以及疲劳前后的杯子搬运任务。在没有目标高度输入的情况下，门控方法在每种负载下均生成了顺序正确的低-中-高参考：目标变化优先分配给轨迹自适应，而增加的负载则将自适应转向能力通道。它在所有九种条件下均取得最低 RMSE（0.017 rad，对比无门控双估计的 0.041 rad 和基于固定参考误差的 AAN 的 0.127 rad）。疲劳后能力估计下降，且在所有高度下辅助均增加，支持了功能可行性。

## Abstract
Assist-as-needed (AAN) control must distinguish between changes in intended motion and changes in effective voluntary torque capability, even though both can produce similar kinematic and interaction responses. A dual-measurand multimodal MHE-MPC architecture is developed as the base framework, in which the intended trajectory and the task-dependent effective voluntary torque capability are estimated online as distinct quantities, and both estimates are used to adapt robotic assistance. Building on this architecture, the ambiguity between the two update channels is formulated as a continuous credit-assignment problem and resolved by an attribution gate. A trajectory-change indicator is obtained from persistent corrective interaction, whereas a capability-change indicator is derived from the residual between normalized muscle activation and dynamics-reconstructed voluntary torque. Nine healthy participants completed factorial target-height-load trials and a pre-post fatigue cup-transport task. Without target-height input, the gated method generated correctly ordered Low-Middle-High references under every load: target changes were preferentially assigned to trajectory adaptation, whereas added load shifted adaptation toward the capability channel. It achieved the lowest RMSE in all nine conditions (0.017 rad versus 0.041 rad for ungated dual estimation and 0.127 rad for fixed-reference error-based AAN). Post-fatigue capability estimates decreased and assistance increased at all heights, supporting functional feasibility.
