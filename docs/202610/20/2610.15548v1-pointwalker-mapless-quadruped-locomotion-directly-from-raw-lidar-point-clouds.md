## 摘要
用于巡检和野外任务部署的四足机器人必须穿越异质地形，包括楼梯、斜坡、间隙、高台、踏脚石和平衡木，而这些地形中的安全支撑稀疏或可透视。现有方法在感知与控制之间插入地形图或深度图像。地图维护成本高，易受里程计漂移和异常值破坏，并会被可透视格栅误导；深度相机则受光照和表面材质影响而性能下降。本文提出PointWalker，一个无地图框架，其单一强化学习策略直接消费来自四个激光雷达（LiDAR）传感器的原始点云。体素化点云经线性自注意力编码，并通过交叉注意力与本体感知融合，使地形几何信息无需地图或落脚点估计即可到达策略。一次近端策略优化（PPO）训练，结合地形感知奖励和可透视格栅的双通道仿真，得到一个适用于全部七类地形的策略，在仿真中平均成功率为90.1%。在Deep Robotics X30上以50 Hz机载零样本部署，它能跨越0.8米间隙、攀爬0.8米平台和45°钢格栅楼梯、承载50公斤有效载荷，并完成2.5公里山路路线。

## Abstract
Quadruped robots deployed for inspection and field missions must cross heterogeneous terrain, from stairs, slopes, and gaps to high platforms, stepping stones, and balance beams, where safe support is sparse or see-through. Existing methods interpose a terrain map or a depth image between sensing and control. Maps are costly to maintain, corrupted by odometry drift and outliers, and misled by see-through gratings, while depth cameras degrade with lighting and surface material. In this paper, we present PointWalker, a mapless framework whose single reinforcement learning policy directly consumes raw point clouds from four light detection and ranging (LiDAR) sensors. Voxelized points are encoded by linear self-attention and fused with proprioception through cross-attention, so terrain geometry reaches the policy without maps or foothold estimation. One proximal policy optimization (PPO) run, with terrain-aware rewards and dual-channel simulation of see-through gratings, yields one policy for all seven terrain classes with a 90.1% mean success rate in simulation. Deployed zero-shot on a Deep Robotics X30 at 50 Hz onboard, it crosses 0.8-m gaps, climbs 0.8-m platforms and 45° steel-grating stairs, carries a 50-kg payload, and completes a 2.5-km mountain route.
