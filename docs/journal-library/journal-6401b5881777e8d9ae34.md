## 中文摘要

机器人可以检测和追踪其环境中的相关物体，其中许多都配备移动摄像头作为主要传感器，处理图像并保持检测物体的内部表示.我们提出了一种新型的活跃视觉记忆，这种视觉内存基于多模式过濾器的组合，有效地集成部分信息.视觉注意子系统分布在负责检测相关物体的软件组件中.我们展示了这种感知系统的效率和鲁棒性在真正的人形机器人参与RoboCupSPL比赛中。

## Abstract

Robots detect and keep track of relevant objects in their environment to accomplish some tasks. Many of them are equipped with mobile cameras as the main sensors, process the images and maintain an internal representation of the detected objects. We propose a novel active visual memory that moves the camera to detect objects in robot&#x27;s surroundings and tracks their positions. This visual memory is based on a combination of multi-modal filters that efficiently integrates partial information. The visual attention subsystem is distributed among the software components in charge of detecting relevant objects. We demonstrate the efficiency and robustness of this perception system in a real humanoid robot participating in the RoboCup SPL competition.
