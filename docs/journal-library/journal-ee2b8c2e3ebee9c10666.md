## 中文摘要

正如人类视觉在设计步行辅助技术的控制策略时在指导适应性运动方面起着基本作用一样，计算机视觉的使用可能会显著改善基于外部环境的辅助调节.在这封信中，我们开发了一台髋关节 外骨骼助力服控制器，该系统由7名健康参与者在由楼梯和平面地面组成的地下路径上进行测试.参与者使用外骨骼助力服禁用 (Exo Off)，持续辅助配置文件 (Vision Off) 和辅助调节 (Vision On) 完成任务.我们的结果表明，控制器能够在实时中对用户前面的路径进行分类，每类的总准确度超过85%，并根据此进行辅助调节.对用户的影响的评估显示，Vision On能够超过其他两个条件:我们比Exo Off获得了显著更高的代谢节约率，当登上楼梯时的峰值是$\\$-20\%$和当整个路径上升或下降时的峰值是$\\$-16%$，而Vision Off则比上升或下降楼梯时的峰值.这种领域的进步可能会导致在现实场景中利用轻量级步行辅助技术取得进展。

## Abstract

Just like in humans vision plays a fundamental role in guiding adaptive locomotion, when designing the control strategy for a walking assistive technology, the use of computer vision may substantially improve modulation of the assistance based on the external environment. In this letter, we developed a hip exosuit controller able to distinguish among three different walking terrains through the use of an RGB camera and to adapt the assistance accordingly. The system was tested with seven healthy participants walking throughout an overground path comprising of staircases and level ground. Subjects performed the task with the exosuit disabled (Exo Off), constant assistance profile (Vision Off), and with assistance modulation (Vision On). Our results showed that the controller was able to classify in real-time the path in front of the user with an overall accuracy per class above the 85%, and to perform assistance modulation accordingly. Evaluation related to the effects on the user showed thatVision Onwas able to outperform the other two conditions: we obtained significantly higher metabolic savings thanExo Off, with a peak of$\approx$$-20\%$when climbing up the staircase and$\approx$$-16\%$in the overall path, and thanVision Offwhen ascending or descending stairs. Such advancements in the field may yield to a step forward for the exploitation of lightweight walking assistive technologies in real-life scenarios.
