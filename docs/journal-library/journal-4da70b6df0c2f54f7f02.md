## 中文摘要

抽象手臂和手指麻痺，例如由于脑干中风，往往导致无法执行日常生活活动 (ADL)，如饮食和饮食.最近表明，混合电脑脑学/电脑脑学 (EEG/EOG) 脑/神经手 外骨骼 可以恢复四肢患者的手功能，但尚不清楚这种控制范式是否可以用于半自主全臂 外骨骼恢复ADL的流动，可靠和安全操作.为了测试这一情况，七名有能力的参与者 (七名右手男性，平均年龄30±8岁) 接受了指令，用电脑电脑/EOG控制的全臂外骨骼 连接到右手臂上，执行多个子任务 (到达，抓住，喝，转移和释放杯子) 的饮用任务.流动可靠的控制被定义为每项子任务在3秒以下的平均启动时间 (TTI) 执行，至少在5秒内成功启动75%的子任务.在使用该系统期间，没有报告任何副作用.所有参与者都能够流动和可靠地控制视觉指导的自主全臂外骨骼 (在各种模式中平均TTI 2.12 ± 0. 78 秒，以EOG 1.9 秒和EEG 控制 4.1 秒达到 75% 的成功初始化)，为严重的手臂和手麻痺恢复ADL铺平了道路。

## Abstract

Abstract Arm and finger paralysis, e.g. due to brain stem stroke, often results in the inability to perform activities of daily living (ADLs) such as eating and drinking. Recently, it was shown that a hybrid electroencephalography/electrooculography (EEG/EOG) brain/neural hand exoskeleton can restore hand function to quadriplegics, but it was unknown whether such control paradigm can be also used for fluent, reliable and safe operation of a semi-autonomous whole-arm exoskeleton restoring ADLs. To test this, seven abled-bodied participants (seven right-handed males, mean age 30 ± 8 years) were instructed to use an EEG/EOG-controlled whole-arm exoskeleton attached to their right arm to perform a drinking task comprising multiple sub-tasks (reaching, grasping, drinking, moving back and releasing a cup). Fluent and reliable control was defined as average ‘time to initialize’ (TTI) execution of each sub-task below 3 s with successful initializations of at least 75% of sub-tasks within 5 s. During use of the system, no undesired side effects were reported. All participants were able to fluently and reliably control the vision-guided autonomous whole-arm exoskeleton (average TTI 2.12 ± 0.78 s across modalities with 75% successful initializations reached at 1.9 s for EOG and 4.1 s for EEG control) paving the way for restoring ADLs in severe arm and hand paralysis.
