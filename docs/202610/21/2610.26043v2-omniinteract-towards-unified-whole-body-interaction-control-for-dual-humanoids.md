## 摘要
物理交互是人形机器人协作的一项关键能力，需要协调的全身运动与精确接触。然而，学习多样化的双人形机器人交互面临两大挑战：（1）人-机器人形态差异会在重定向过程中破坏接触接口和空间关系；（2）联合学习运动技能与伙伴协调使得在交互多样性增长时难以保持控制保真度。本文提出 OmniInteract，通过表面感知建模和可扩展残差策略学习来应对这些挑战。首先，我们引入表面接触感知重定向（Surface Contact-Aware Retargeting），利用密集的人-机器人表面对应关系对两个双人形机器人进行联合重定向，从而保留显式交互接触接口及其近场几何上下文。其次，我们提出一种可扩展的基于流的交互策略（Scalable Flow-based Interaction Policy），用于以伙伴为条件的残差生成。它将双人形机器人控制分解为可复用的运动基础与交互特定适配：伙伴感知专家在冻结的全身运动先验上学习修正，而条件流匹配在共享残差动作空间中将它们统一起来。这能够在保留预训练运动能力的同时实现可扩展的技能整合。大量实验表明，OmniInteract 提高了重定向保真度和双人形机器人协调性，并在技能多样性增长时更好地保持交互性能。真实世界部署进一步展示了成功的接触建立、稳定的全身协调以及从外部扰动中恢复的能力。

## Abstract
Physical interaction is a key capability for humanoid collaboration, requiring coordinated whole-body motion and precise contact. However, learning diverse dual-humanoid interactions faces two major challenges: (1) human-robot morphological differences disrupt contact interfaces and spatial relationships during retargeting, and (2) jointly learning motor skills and partner coordination makes it difficult to retain control fidelity as interaction diversity grows. In this paper, we propose OmniInteract to address these challenges through surface-aware modeling and scalable residual policy learning. First, we introduce Surface Contact-Aware Retargeting, which leverages dense human-robot surface correspondences to jointly retarget both humanoids, preserving explicit interaction contact interfaces and their near-field geometric context. Second, we propose a Scalable Flow-based Interaction Policy for partner-conditioned residual generation. It factorizes dual-humanoid control into a reusable motor foundation and interaction-specific adaptations: partner-aware experts learn corrections over a frozen whole-body motion prior, while conditional flow matching unifies them in a shared residual action space. This enables scalable skill consolidation while retaining pretrained motor capabilities. Extensive experiments demonstrate that OmniInteract improves retargeting fidelity and dual-humanoid coordination while better retaining interaction performance as skill diversity grows. Real-world deployment further demonstrates successful contact establishment, stable whole-body coordination, and recovery from external perturbations.
