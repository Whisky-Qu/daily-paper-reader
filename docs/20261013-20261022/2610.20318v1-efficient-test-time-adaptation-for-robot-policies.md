## 摘要
用于机器人运动的强化学习策略在部署时若运行条件发生变化（例如崎岖或湿滑地形、负载变化、传感退化），可能会失效；而在设备端时延和内存约束下，重新训练或人工重校准通常不可行。我们研究机器人控制中的测试时适应（TTA）：在执行过程中以自监督方式在线适应预训练策略，且无法获取奖励。我们的起点是一个轻量级基线：仅更新 actor，并将冻结的预训练 critic 作为部署时的性能代理。在此基础上，我们提出 TEMPO，一种测试时熵正则化、记忆引导的策略优化方法，可在严重分布偏移下稳定由 critic 驱动的更新。TEMPO 结合了：(i) 对 critic 预测分布施加促进熵的正则项，以缓解过度自信的外推；(ii) 受优先回放启发的基于分数的记忆机制，该机制优先关注低价值（“困难”）观测，并应用重要性采样，将有限计算集中于最具信息量的部署条件。在 MuJoCo Playground 中的 Go1、Go2 和 G1 上，在四种代表性分布偏移和多种架构下，TEMPO 均带来一致增益，在单设备设置下提升最高达 80%。我们进一步在加装小腿负载的实体 Unitree Go2 上验证，完全设备端适应在真实世界多个回合中相较未适应基线提升了性能。

## Abstract
Reinforcement-learning policies for robot locomotion can fail at deployment when operating conditions shift (e.g., rough or slippery terrain, payload changes, sensing degradation), and retraining or manual recalibration is often infeasible under on-device latency and memory constraints. We study test-time adaptation (TTA) for robotic control: adapting a pretrained policy online and in a self-supervised manner during execution, without access to rewards. Our starting point is a lightweight baseline that updates only the actor using a frozen pretrained critic as a deployment-time performance surrogate. Building on this, we introduce TEMPO, a test-time entropy-regularized, memory-guided policy optimization method that stabilizes critic-driven updates under severe shift. TEMPO combines (i) an entropy-promoting regularizer on the critic's predictive distribution to mitigate overconfident extrapolation, and (ii) a score-based memory, inspired by prioritized replay, that prioritizes low-value ("hard") observations and applies importance sampling to focus limited computation on the most informative deployment conditions. Across Go1, Go2, and G1 in MuJoCo Playground under four representative shifts and multiple architectures, TEMPO yields consistent gains, reaching up to 80% improvement in a single-device setting. We further validate on a physical Unitree Go2 with an added calf payload, where fully on-device adaptation improves performance over the non-adapted baseline across real-world episodes.
